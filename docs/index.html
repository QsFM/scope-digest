  5. 5Physics consistent conditional flow matching model as a plasma physics surrogate model

    초록Abstract Physics-informed surrogate modeling for steady-state systems often struggles with data scarcity and optimization instabilities from soft penalty losses. Unlike prevailing physics-informed generative methods that rely on explicit, differentiable PDE residuals during training, our framework targets systems where closed-form governing equations are unavailable or non-differentiable. We introduce a unified continuous-time Conditional Flow Matching (CFM) framework for surrogate modeling and sparse data assimilation, evaluated on low-temperature plasma dynamics. By combining data-dependent interpolants with dynamic masking, our approach handles unconditioned prediction and missing-data imputation within a single architecture. We replace standard velocity matching with a direct state-prediction ($y$-prediction) objective, eliminating variance-induced sampling inefficiencies in deterministic regimes.
